Delete Your Account & Data

TrueNorth: Citizenship Test lets you permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time.

Option 1: Delete it yourself in the app (fastest)

  1. Open the TrueNorth: Citizenship Test app and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings (bottom of the Home screen menu).
  3. In the Account section, tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm you want to delete your account. You may be asked to re-enter your password to confirm your identity.

This immediately and permanently deletes your account and data. It cannot be undone.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you can't access the app, email us at t.north.apps@gmail.com from the email address associated with your account, with the subject line "Delete My Account." We'll process your request within a reasonable time.

What gets deleted

  • Your account (email address) in Firebase Authentication.
  • Your practice test usage record, favourites, and flashcard progress stored in Firebase Cloud Firestore.

Your locally-stored quiz history and progress (which never leaves your device) is also cleared when you delete your account in the app. If you request deletion by email instead, this local data remains only on your own device until you uninstall the app.

Question reports are the one exception. If you've used "Report an issue" on any question, that report (question ID, reason, and note) is kept after your account is deleted, so we can still follow up on content issues — it's just no longer linked to a live account. Email us if you'd like a specific report removed too.

Note: Deleting your account does not automatically cancel or refund any pass you've purchased. Purchases are managed separately through Google Play — see your Google Play subscriptions and purchases page.
