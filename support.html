Common questions

How do I reset my password?
On the sign-in screen, tap "Forgot password?" and enter your email. You'll get a reset link.

How do I delete my account?
Open the app, go to Settings, and tap "Delete Account." This permanently removes your account and saved progress. If you can't access the app, email us and we'll handle it.

I found a wrong answer in a question.
Tap "Report an issue" on any question and tell us what's wrong. We review every report.

What do the passes include?
Flash cards, progress tracking, the test guide, and one practice test each day are free. A prepaid pass unlocks unlimited practice tests, Favourite Questions, and Smart Review. Passes are one-time payments and do not auto-renew.
