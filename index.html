Privacy Policy for TrueNorth: Citizenship Test

Last updated: July 27, 2026

TrueNorth: Citizenship Test ("the app") is developed by an independent developer, not affiliated with or endorsed by the Government of Canada or Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC). This policy explains what information the app collects, how it's used, and your choices.

Information We Collect

  • Email address. When you create an account, your email address is collected and stored by Firebase Authentication (a service provided by Google) to let you sign in. We do not see or store your password ourselves — that is handled securely by Firebase.
  • Practice test usage count. To enforce the free-tier limit (a small number of free practice tests before a paid pass is required), we store a count of completed tests linked to your account in Firebase Cloud Firestore (also a Google service).
  • Quiz results and progress. Your practice test scores, answers, and category-by-category progress are stored only locally on your device and are not uploaded to us or any server. Uninstalling the app deletes this data.
  • Purchase information. If you buy a paid pass, the purchase is processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your payment card details — only a confirmation that a purchase was made.

What We Don't Do

We do not show ads, use third-party analytics or advertising trackers, collect your location, access your contacts, camera, or microphone, or sell your data to anyone.

How We Use Your Information

Your email and usage count are used solely to operate your account and enforce the free-test limit described above. We do not use your information for any other purpose.

Data Sharing

We use Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore) and Google Play Billing, both provided by Google, to operate the app. These providers process data on our behalf under their own privacy and security terms. We do not otherwise share your information with third parties.

Data Retention and Deletion

Your account data is retained until you delete it. You can permanently delete your account and all associated data (your email address and usage records) at any time directly in the app: go to Settings → Account → Delete Account. This immediately and permanently removes your account from Firebase Authentication and your data from Firebase Cloud Firestore. This action cannot be undone.

If you're unable to access the app, you can request deletion the same way by visiting this page or emailing us at the address below.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under applicable data protection law (such as the EU/UK GDPR, or other regional privacy laws) to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you;
  • Correct inaccurate data;
  • Request deletion of your data (see above for the fastest way to do this);
  • Request a copy of your data in a portable format;
  • Object to or restrict certain processing;
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the email below. We'll respond within a reasonable time and in any case within the timeframe required by applicable law.

Children's Privacy

This app is intended for adults preparing for the Canadian citizenship test and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new "Last updated" date.
